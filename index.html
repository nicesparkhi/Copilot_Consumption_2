Insights & Anomalies · Copilot Platform
🔴 Default Environment at 241% — Unchanged
Nice Systems Ltd. (default) consumed 240,701 credits against an entitlement of 100,000. This 141% overage is the single largest cost driver and has not been resolved since the prior report. Action required: expand the billing plan or migrate workloads to NiCEAllPROD which has 25% headroom remaining.
🔴 Flow 8a4fbd54 Still Growing — +8,170 Credits
The unnamed Power Automate flow grew from 140,030 to 148,200 billed credits this period. With no display name or owner, governance is impossible. A second unnamed flow (475f2882) adds another 12,062 billed credits. Combined, these two flows account for 160,262 billed credits with zero accountability.
⚠️ Procurement Buyer Agent — Response Surge +29%
Procurement Buyer Agent responses jumped from 772 to 1,000 (+29%) in one period. It runs on claude-opus-4-6 — the highest-cost model — and is used by 22 licensed + 3 unlicensed users. Growing usage on premium models needs cost monitoring. Consider routing routine queries to a standard model.
⚠️ SharePoint Channel Credits Down 34%
SharePoint channel credits dropped from 91,402 to 59,978 (−34%). This could indicate reduced SharePoint-based agent usage or a configuration change. Key agents like Evidencentral Knowledge Neo use SharePoint for grounding — if their consumption fell this may explain the drop. Worth investigating whether grounding quality degraded.
ℹ️ Premium LLM Spend Trending Down (−6.5%)
Total premium model credits fell from 813K to 760K (−6.5%). claude-opus-4-6 dropped from 446K to 399K. This is positive cost news — but gpt-5-reasoning increased from 144K to 152K, driven by Ask Rovo. The overall mix is healthier; however the unnamed Flow 8a4fbd54 partially offsets these gains.
ℹ️ NiCE Copilot Org Agent — 2,759 Responses (+21%)
NiCE Copilot (org-built) delivered 2,759 responses across 123 users (13 licensed + 110 unlicensed) — up 21% from 2,288. This is the platform's most widely-used org agent and shows healthy internal adoption. The unlicensed user majority (110) means consumption is charged differently — worth confirming billing model intent.
✅ Active User Rate Stable at 69.6%
1,566 of 2,250 licensed users submitted at least one prompt in the 7-day window. The top 10 users each submitted 243–467 prompts. Work-mode Copilot Chat remains dominant at 68.5% of prompts. Krystel Alvarez (#1 at 467 prompts) is entirely work-mode — a strong signal of enterprise task automation rather than casual browsing.
✅ Agent Ecosystem Growth — 36,142 Responses
4,921 users interacted with agents (up from 4,904), generating 36,142 total responses (+4%). Sabrina Pickering used 14 different agents — the broadest portfolio in the tenant. The 15 org-built agents punch above their weight: 14.3% of responses from just 0.6% of agents, proving curated org agents outperform the long tail of user-created ones.

Insights & Anomalies · Cowork Platform
🔴 Single-User Concentration Risk
Vasily Derjavets accounts for 1,024 of 1,356 tasks — 75.5% of all Cowork activity. This period his task count fell slightly (from 1,053) but concentration remains extreme. If this user churns or pauses automation, overall platform metrics collapse. The second-highest user (Parit Patel, 69 tasks) is 15× smaller.
⚠️ 14 Users With Zero Engagement
31.1% of provisioned users have never run a single task. Last logins range from April–May 2026. These users have now been inactive for 2–3 months. A targeted re-engagement campaign — a personal onboarding session or a pre-built starter task — could convert even 5 users and meaningfully grow platform depth.
⚠️ Low Session Depth — Most Users Use 1–5 Days
27 of 31 active users engaged for just 1–5 days. Only 3 users show sustained engagement (Lee Romrell 28 days, Vasily 17 days, Hadar 15 days). Average active days is 3.7 — meaning most users try Cowork once or twice and don't return. Recurring scheduled tasks (vs one-off manual tasks) are the key differentiator for sticky users.
ℹ️ Automation Rate at 78.6%
Nearly 4 in 5 tasks are scheduled automations. The top automation champions — Vasily (92%), Parit Patel (94%), Alla Gazit (100%), Tom Yang (100%), Richard Moore (100%) — show the platform's highest-value use pattern. Manual-only users (23 out of 31) are an untapped opportunity to introduce scheduled workflows.
ℹ️ Recent Activity Improving — 24 Users in Last 7 Days
24 of 45 users (53.3%) were active in the last 7 days — up from 21 in the prior report. This is the strongest recent-activity signal since onboarding. The upward trend suggests either organic adoption growth or scheduled automations running more frequently for existing users.
✅ Manual User Volume Slightly Up (+4%)
User-initiated tasks grew from 279 to 290 (+4%) this period, driven by Hadar Adlan (37 tasks, up from 35) and Asia Ashmoz Hatukay (20 tasks). This suggests manual interactive usage is growing alongside automation — a healthy sign that users are actively engaging with Cowork rather than just relying on background scheduled runs.
