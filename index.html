Why it matters

B2E (employee-facing) agent interactions by M365 Copilot–licensed users are generally zero-rated. Credits consumed by unlicensed users (or autonomous triggers) bill against prepaid capacity or pay-as-you-go — so unlicensed consumption is the primary lever for both cost and licence-assignment decisions.
