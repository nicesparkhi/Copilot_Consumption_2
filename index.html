Insights & Anomalies · Automated Analysis
🔴 Critical: Default Environment Overage
Nice Systems Ltd. (default) consumed 240,701 credits against an entitlement of 100,000 — a 140.7% overage. This is the single largest driver of overuse. Recommend reviewing billing policy and expanding entitlement or migrating workloads to NiCEAllPROD.
🔴 Unnamed Power Automate Flows
Two unidentified flows consumed large credits: flow 8a4fbd54 (140,030 billed) and flow 475f2882 (12,062 billed). Neither has a display name or owner. Action required: identify owners via Power Platform admin center and assign governance policies immediately.
⚠️ June 4 Spike — PAYG Triggered
On June 4 total consumption hit 75,566 credits — 4.4× the daily average (17,034) and triggered PAYG charges of 12,468 credits on NiCE-All-PROD. Pattern indicates a batch process or bulk test run. Implement daily consumption alerts to detect this earlier.
⚠️ claude-opus-4-6 Dominance (55%)
Premium LLM spend is concentrated in claude-opus-4-6 at 446,054 credits (55%) of all model usage. Key consumers: Evidencentral Knowledge Neo, Procurement Buyer Agent, and LearnLoop. Consider downgrading routine queries to Sonnet or gpt-5-chat to reduce cost.
ℹ️ Strong Copilot Adoption — 71.5% Active
1,608 of 2,250 licensed users submitted at least one prompt in the 7-day window — above-average for enterprise deployments. Top 10 users submitted 250–482 prompts each. nice.com (1,571 users) leads over niceactimize.com (678 users). Work-mode chat preferred at 68% of all prompts.
ℹ️ Agent Ecosystem Scale — 4,904 Users
4,904 unique users interacted with declarative agents vs 2,250 M365 activity users — a large agent-only user base. Sabrina Pickering used 15 different agents. The 720 multi-agent users represent power adopters driving disproportionate value. User-created agents dominate at 74% of responses.
✅ NiCE-All-PROD Entitlement Expansion
NiCE-All-PROD entitlement was proactively expanded from 95,000 to 115,998 credits around June 16. Total utilization on this environment is 75% — healthy headroom. This shows governance awareness and proactive capacity management.
✅ Org-Built Agent Efficiency
Only 15 org-built agents generated 5,872 responses — far higher per-agent rate than the 2,539 user-created agents (24,860 responses). NiCE Copilot (2,288) and NiCE GPT (2,241) top the charts, serving the broadest user bases and delivering maximum ROI per agent.

Insights & Anomalies · Cowork Platform Analysis
🔴 Single-User Concentration Risk
Vasily Derjavets alone accounts for 1,053 of 1,387 total tasks — 75.9% of all Cowork activity. This extreme concentration means platform health metrics are driven by one power user. If this user churns or changes usage, overall statistics will collapse. Recommend identifying and nurturing additional automation adopters.
⚠️ 14 Users With Zero Engagement
31.1% of provisioned users (14 of 45) have never executed a single task. Their last logins were April–May 2026 — meaning 1–2 months of inactivity. These are strong churn candidates. Targeted re-engagement (training, use-case demos, or a scheduled welcome task) could convert even 5 of these users and meaningfully grow platform adoption.
⚠️ Low Session Depth — Avg 3.7 Active Days
Among active users, the average number of active days is only 3.7, and 27 of 31 active users used the platform for just 1–5 days. Only 3 users (Lee Romrell at 28 days, Vasily at 19, Hadar at 14) show sustained engagement. The platform has good breadth but shallow depth — most users try it once and don't return regularly.
ℹ️ Automation-Driven Platform (79.9%)
Nearly 4 in 5 tasks are scheduled automations, not manual user actions. This is healthy for a productivity platform — users are setting up recurring workflows rather than one-off interactions. The top automation champions — Vasily Derjavets (92%), Parit Patel (93%), Alla Gazit (100%), Tom Yang (100%) — are getting the most ROI from scheduled workflows.
ℹ️ Lee Romrell — High Engagement, Low Output
Lee Romrell-Finlinson logged the most active days of any user (28 days) but executed only 6 tasks — zero scheduled. This pattern suggests daily platform logins for consumption or review rather than task creation. This user may benefit from guidance on setting up scheduled automations to convert their high engagement into productive output.
✅ Strong Recent Activity — 21 Users Active in 7 Days
21 of 45 users (46.7%) were active within the last 7 days as of June 30, 2026. Combined with 10 active within 8–30 days, 31 users (68.9%) have recent Cowork sessions. This signals the platform is alive and in daily use, not just a pilot deployment. The automation-heavy nature (Vasily, Parit, Alla, Tom) sustains high task counts even during quiet periods.
